01 — About

Curious about how systems break, and how to build them better.

I'm an Honours Computer Science student at North-West University (Mafikeng Campus), where I also work as a Student Assistant — tutoring undergraduates in Introduction to Computing & Programming, Artificial Intelligence, and Computer Networks.

My Honours research looks at IoT security: assessing how well published vulnerability frameworks hold up when tested against a simulated smart building. Alongside it I build full-stack applications and lead small project teams. I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Electronics in 2026, and currently publishing my first research paper.
